The place pack already carries exactly what this needs. SF Public Works' real street sweeping schedule for your block — east side Mondays (1st & 3rd, noon–2pm) and west side Thursdays (1st, 3rd, 5th, noon–2pm) — plus the holiday exceptions SFMTA tracks separately. Rather than inventing a schedule, the tool can read this live from the block's own feed (`block.json?where=46-moraga`), so if the city changes it or a neighbor corrects it, your reminder stays right.

And the history is real too — not generic Sunset trivia but things pinned to this stretch: Henry Doelger's Freedom House and Rainbow House going up nearby in '41–'42 right before wartime building stopped, the Noriega seawall and the sand crews that still move 30,000 cubic yards of it some Junes, the clipper ship King Philip buried under the beach a third of a mile southwest, and the fact that the 1906 earthquake's rupture began almost straight out from here. Each with its own source to credit — [OpenSFHistory](https://opensfhistory.org), [SF Planning's Sunset survey](https://default.sfplanning.org/Preservation/sunset_survey/Adopted_SunsetHCS.pdf), [NPS Golden Gate](https://home.nps.gov/goga/learn/historyculture/vestiges-shipwrecks.htm).

Nothing in the pack duplicates this exact idea — the closest things are the plain city schedule and the [Sunset Board](https://sunsetboard.org/add) flyer board, neither of which pairs a reminder with a fact. Worth a quick nod to whoever's the first person you'd want to see it — maybe just a neighbor across the street, since the east/west split means half the block cares about Mondays and half about Thursdays.
