This is a wonderful way to turn a chore into a moment of connection. By pairing a "move your car" alert with a "did you know" about the block, you're transforming a potential frustration into a reason to feel rooted in the Outer Sunset.

The neighborhood commons already has the data you need for this: the city's street sweeping schedule for 46th Ave (Moraga to Noriega) and a rich set of local history, from the 1940s "Rainbow House" to the clipper ship *King Philip* buried under the sand.

Before you build, you might want to reach out to **Sandi Lam Harder** or **Josh Nesbit** from [The Sunset Wave](/gallery?item=repo:people:the-sunset-wave). They are organizing block-level gatherings for March 2027; a tool like this is a perfect "bridge" to get neighbors talking and coordinated before a big event like that.

Since this is a utility that needs to be useful in three seconds, we should decide how neighbors actually get the reminder.
