## What the place found

The commons already has the street cleaning schedule for 46th Ave, Moraga to Noriega — east side is Mon 1st & 3rd noon–2pm, west side is Thu 1st, 3rd, 5th noon–2pm, with the city's holiday exceptions. It also holds real history about the block: Freedom House and Rainbow House from 1941–42, the Noriega seawall and its sand drifts, the King Philip clipper ship under the sand, and the 1906 earthquake's origin point nearby. There's also a Sunset Board where neighbors post flyers, and a social club (Sunset Social Club) that organizes block gatherings.

**Who to talk to first:** The Sunset Social Club stewards (Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit) already host block gatherings; they'd be a natural partner for getting the word out. The street cleaning schedule itself lives on SF Public Works.
