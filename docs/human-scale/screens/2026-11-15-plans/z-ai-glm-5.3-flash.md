This is a great idea for the 46th Ave block in the Outer Sunset. Having a clear, reliable schedule is so practical, and pairing it with the history of the block—like the stories of the Noriega seawall or the King Philip ship—really grounds the neighborhood and helps people feel connected to the place.

Since we are working with the block's existing knowledge base, we can shape this project in a few ways:

1.  **The Automated Reminder:** A simple, recurring notification (via email or text) that pings neighbors on the scheduled street cleaning days with the time and a rotating historical fact from the block's pinned notes.
2.  **The Block History Hub:** A central, public web page (like a digital bulletin board) where the street cleaning schedule is always visible, but the historical facts rotate daily so neighbors can browse the block's story whenever they want.
3.  **The Outreach Campaign:** A print-ready series of flyers or a neighborhood newsletter that you distribute physically, using the digital data to keep the information accurate and shareable with those who don't use the internet.
