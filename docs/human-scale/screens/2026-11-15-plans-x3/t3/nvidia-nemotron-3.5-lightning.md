The place's commons for 46-moraga already has the street cleaning schedule (East side: Mon 1st & 3rd, noon to 2pm; West side: Thu 1st, 3rd, 5th, noon to 2pm, per SF Public Works), and a rich set of block history pinned to it: Freedom House and Rainbow House (1941-1942, patriotic model-home names from developer Henry Doelger's Sunset era), the Noriega seawall and its annual sand drifts (1980s, 2023 crews moved ~30,000 cubic yards of sand toward the ocean), and the King Philip clipper ship buried in Ocean Beach sand (wrecked 1878, timbers resurfacing periodically). Old photographs are available on OpenSFHistory. 

People to talk to first: Sunset Social Club stewards Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit (who organize block gatherings); Surfrider Foundation SF (beach cleanups, ocean advocacy); Sunset Board (flyers on poles and boards). The block also connects to wider Sunset rhythms: Sundays at the Beach - Tai Chi (3rd Sundays), The Sunset Wave (spring equinox block gatherings), and the Outer Sunset Farmers' Market (Sunday mornings).
