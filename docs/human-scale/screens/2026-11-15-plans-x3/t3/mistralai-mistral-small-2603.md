## Three directions we could take this

- **SMS reminders** — a simple one-way text service that sends the next cleaning day and a local history fact to anyone who signs up. No app, no login, just a text to their phone.
- **A landing page** — a single web page that shows the next cleaning day and the rotating history fact, with an optional sign-up for email or SMS reminders. Neighbors can bookmark it or share the link.
- **A block hub page** — a small civic info hub for your block that shows the cleaning schedule, the history facts, and links to local groups like the Sunset Wave and Surfrider. It becomes the place to go for block info.
