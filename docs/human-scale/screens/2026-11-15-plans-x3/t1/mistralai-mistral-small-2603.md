46th Ave, Moraga to Noriega — that’s a block with stories to tell and a rhythm the city keeps. The city’s schedule is set: east side sweeps Mon 1st & 3rd, west side Thu 1st/3rd/5th, both noon–2pm, not on holidays. And the block’s history is right there in the commons: Freedom House and Rainbow House from Doelger’s 1941–42 wave, the King Philip’s hull under the sand near Noriega, the seawall’s shifting sands. Old photos of the block are pinned too.

## Three directions this could go

- **One-way broadcast**: A weekly text or email list that sends a simple reminder (“west side, Thu 1st, noon–2pm”) plus one true fact about the block’s history. Low lift, easy to start, but it only goes one way.
- **Two-way co-op board**: A shared board (digital or cork) where neighbors can post reminders, swap care tasks, and add their own notes, anchored by the weekly street cleaning reminder and the historical fact. More relational, but needs a little upkeep.
- **Block ritual**: A weekly “street cleaning + stories” moment — neighbors gather at a corner or porch at cleaning time, share the reminder and a fact, and connect. The tool is a flyer/invitation that makes this ritual visible and repeatable. Highest relational payoff, but it asks neighbors to show up together.
