The block's commons already has the bones of this: the city publishes the sweeping schedule for 46th Ave (east side Mon 1st & 3rd, west side Thu 1st/3rd/5th, noon–2pm, skipping holidays), and there's real history pinned to your block — the Doelger model homes era, the King Philip clipper ship buried under the sand near Noriega, the 1906 earthquake starting just offshore. So the tool doesn't need anyone to type dates or facts in; it can read both live, with sources credited on screen.

One thing worth doing before or while building: the [Sunset Wave](https://thesunsetwave.org) — Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit of the Sunset Social Club — is organizing neighbor gatherings on blocks across the Sunset, and a street-cleaning-plus-history reminder is exactly the kind of thing a block gathering could adopt and keep alive. Worth an email to oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com, or just showing a neighbor on 46th the idea over the fence.

A few things shape the build:

## Question for you
1. How should the reminder reach you and the neighbors?
   - A one-page site: today's status, next sweep days, today's history fact — you text the link around
   - Email or text reminders the day before each sweep
   - A printable card for doorways and the corkboard
   - Start with the page, add reminders once neighbors are using it
2. Which side of 46th are you on? (The sweep days differ.)
   - East side — Mondays, 1st & 3rd
   - West side — Thursdays, 1st/3rd/5th
   - Show both sides
3. What should it look like?
   - A hand-painted curb sign — fog-gray ground #E8E4DC, ink navy #1B3A5C, one sunset-orange signal #E07A3F, soft hand-lettered feel
   - A tide-table card from the bait shop — cream paper #F5EFE0, near-black ink #222222, one red flag #C0392B for sweep days, square corners
   - A 1941 Doelger realtor flyer — butter yellow #F7E8B5, warm brown ink #4A3728, sturdy serif type, like the model-home era your block was built in

An attached photo works too if you have something you love the look of.
