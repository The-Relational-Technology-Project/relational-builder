Before we shape this, here's what the Outer Sunset commons already has for your block:

- **The city's schedule** is already published. The app can read it live rather than make neighbors look it up. The [SF street sweeping schedule](https://sfpublicworks.org/services/mechanical-street-sweeping-and-street-cleaning-schedule) covers 46th Ave between Moraga and Noriega: east side Mondays (1st and 3rd), west side Thursdays (1st, 3rd, and 5th), noon to 2pm, not on city holidays. The [SFMTA holiday list](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/drive-park/holiday-enforcement-schedule) tells you when it's suspended.
- **The block's history** is already pinned to it: the 1941 Doelger houses on 42nd and 43rd, the King Philip clipper ship buried near Noriega, and the 1906 earthquake's starting point offshore. The brief also links four old photographs from OpenSFHistory, which you can credit by name. Every reminder can rotate through these.
- **People to talk to:** the Sunset Wave organizers (Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit, through the Sunset Social Club) are gathering neighbors on every block on March 20, 2027, which is a natural moment to launch this. The [Sunset Board](https://sunsetboard.org/add) is where neighbors post flyers. I'd talk to them before building.
- **A precedent:** [The Claypole Commons](/gallery?item=kb_story%3A7f6a4286-e9b8-4f90-ab43-45ac834e0a0f) is a hand-designed print newsletter for 25 houses on one block. It's a good model for a reminder that feels like it belongs to the block, not to an app.
