## Question for you
1. How should the reminder reach a neighbor?
   - A page they bookmark — they open it, see the next cleaning day and a fact, done
   - A page + a calendar file (.ics) they add to their phone — the phone buzzes on cleaning day, the page has the fact
   - A page + email on the morning of — needs a small backend, but the fact lands in their inbox
2. Which of these does it look like?
   - The hand-painted "STREET CLEANING" sign on the pole — deep asphalt grey ground (#2d2d2d), chalk-white lettering, one orange signal stripe (#e85d04) for the date, square corners, dense and direct
   - A page from the Sunset's old neighborhood newspaper — warm cream paper (#f5f0e8), ink navy text (#1a2744), one muted red flag color (#9b2c2c) for the fact, serif type, soft edges, airy
   - A field note card from a park ranger's clipboard — pale sage ground (#e8ede5), dark green ink (#2d4a32), one earthy ochre (#b8860b) for the history fact, monospace type, square corners, compact
