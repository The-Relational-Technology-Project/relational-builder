The block's own commons already has the bones of this: the city publishes 46th Ave's sweeping schedule (east side Mon 1st & 3rd, west side Thu 1st/3rd/5th, noon–2pm, skipping holidays), and the block notes carry real history to pair with it — Doelger's Rainbow House and Freedom House model homes from 1941–42 right between Moraga and Noriega, the King Philip clipper ship buried under the sand near the foot of Noriega, the 1906 quake starting offshore from here. There's also a person worth a quick hello before building: Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit, stewards of [The Sunset Wave](https://thesunsetwave.org) — they're organizing block-by-block neighbor gatherings and would love that this exists, and might know which neighbors on 46th would actually use it.

The idea itself is lovely and small: a reminder that arrives when it matters, carrying one true thing about the street you live on. A few things shape the build:

## Question for you
1. How should the reminder reach people?
   - A simple web page neighbors check or bookmark
   - Text/email reminders they sign up for once
   - A page plus a printable flyer with the schedule for the porch/window
2. Who is it for?
   - Just our block — both sides of 46th, Moraga to Noriega
   - Our block first, easy for other blocks to copy later
3. What should it look like?
   - A hand-lettered sign taped to a porch window — cream paper (#F5EFE0), ink navy (#1E2A3A), one fog-orange signal color (#D97B3F), soft edges
   - A city notice, but friendly — cool white (#FAFAF7), SF municipal navy (#0B3D5C), one red flag red (#C0392B), square corners, stamped look
   - A beach zine page — sand gray (#E8E4DC), near-black chunky type, tide-pool teal (#2E7D74), hard edges

An attached photo or screenshot works too if you have a look in mind.
