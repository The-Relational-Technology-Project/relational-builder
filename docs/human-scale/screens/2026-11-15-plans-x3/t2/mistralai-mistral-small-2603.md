46th Avenue between Moraga and Noriega — that block has a real story to tell. The city sweeps the east side on the 1st and 3rd Mondays at noon, and the west side on the 1st, 3rd, and 5th Thursdays at noon. And the history pinned to it is vivid: Doelger’s Freedom House and Rainbow House went up here in 1941–42, the King Philip clipper lies buried under the sand near Noriega, and the seawall at Noriega still fights the dunes every year.

## Three directions this could go

- **A simple SMS/email reminder** just for your block, sent the night before the sweep, with the day’s true fact about 46th Ave baked in. Low-tech, high-reach, and something you can set up today with a free tool.

- **A one-page flyer** you print and tack to the corner store bulletin board (or share as a PDF) — the next street-cleaning day for each side of the block, plus a short local history line. It looks like a civic bulletin board post: cool white, ink navy, one red accent, square corners.

- **A tiny web page** that shows the next sweep day for your side of the block, plus the day’s historical fact, and lets neighbors bookmark it or subscribe to reminders. It’s a hand-painted sign turned digital: deep green ground, soft edges, painted lettering.
