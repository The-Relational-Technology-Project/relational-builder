The block's commons already carries what you need: the street cleaning schedule (east side 1st & 3rd Mondays, west side 1st, 3rd, 5th Thursdays, noon to 2, off on city holidays), three history facts pinned right on or near 46th between Moraga and Noriega — the Doelger model homes (Freedom House and Rainbow House, 1941–42), the King Philip clipper ship buried under the sand at Noriega and Ortega, and the 1906 earthquake's starting point in the ocean just south of here — plus four old photographs on OpenSFHistory. The app can read all of that live from the block's data feed, so a neighbor's correction reaches it.

One person worth talking to before or while you build: **Sandi Lam Harder** or **Josh Nesbit**, stewards of [The Sunset Wave](https://thesunsetwave.org) — block gatherings across the Sunset on March 20, 2027. A street-cleaning reminder is exactly the kind of small block-level tool that makes a block feel like it already knows itself, and they're the people who think about what a block needs.

Now, "reminder" can take a few shapes, and the right one depends on how your block actually talks to each other:

## Question for you
1. How should the reminder reach people on the block?
   - A page they bookmark or drop in the group chat — shows the next cleaning day and a history fact, no login, no backend, works today
   - A message that posts to the block's group chat automatically on cleaning mornings (small backend, a schedule)
   - An email or text that goes out the morning of cleaning day (backend + a short opt-in list)
2. Which of these does it look like?
   - A hand-painted sign on a wooden post — deep ocean blue ground `#1a3a4a`, cream lettering `#f5f0e8`, one warm signal orange `#e8723a` for the date, soft rounded edges, Playfair Display over Source Sans 3
   - The old SF street cleaning sign itself — white ground `#fafafa`, black ink `#1a1a1a`, the city's standard red `#cc0000` for "move your car," square corners, Oswald over Inter
   - A postcard from the 1940s — warm cream paper `#f5edd6`, dark brown ink `#3d2b1f`, a faded teal accent `#4a8a8a` for the history fact, soft rounded edges, Zilla Slab over Nunito Sans
