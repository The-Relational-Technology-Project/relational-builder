Since you're focusing on your specific block, it might be worth chatting with Sandi Lam Harder or Josh Nesbit from [The Sunset Wave](/gallery?item=repo:people:the-sunset-wave); they're already organizing neighbor gatherings on blocks across the Sunset and might have a great way to share this with your neighbors.

Depending on how you want this to live in the neighborhood, it could take a few shapes:
- **A "Check the Curb" page**: A simple, single-screen site neighbors bookmark to see if today is a cleaning day and read the current history fact.
- **A "Block Alert" signup**: A landing page where neighbors sign up to receive a text or email reminder that includes the history fact.
- **A "Digital Block Board"**: A visual dashboard showing the upcoming cleaning dates and a rotating gallery of old photos from OpenSFHistory.
