## 1. Intent

This work exists so that neighbors can strengthen the social fabric of their communities. It was created by practitioners, not for a market. This license ensures the recipes stay available to the people they were written for, while preventing their enclosure by commercial platforms, consulting firms, or AI products.

The guiding principle: **if your use brings these practices closer to a neighborhood, you are welcome. If your use extracts value from them without reciprocating to communities, you need permission.**

## 3. Freedoms — what is always allowed

The following uses require no permission, no fee, and no approval process. You simply do them.

- **Read, share, print, and distribute** the Work in any format.
- **Fork, adapt, translate, and localize** the Work for your community.
- **Use the Work in Community Use contexts**, including selling printed booklets, zines, or other physical materials at any price.
- **Host local events, gatherings, and workshops** using the Work, whether or not participants pay to attend, as long as the event is organized by a community-based group (not a commercial services firm).
- **Quote, cite, and reference** the Work in journalism, academic research, and criticism.

## 4. Restrictions — what requires permission

The following uses require **written permission from the Steward** before proceeding. Permission may be granted freely, conditionally, or denied. Contact the Steward at the address listed in Section 7.

- **Commercial Service Use.** Incorporating recipes into paid workshops, consulting engagements, training programs, or professional services requires permission. This includes solo practitioners, firms of any size, and independent consultants.
- **Platform Use.** Making the Work available through a commercial digital platform, app, or SaaS product requires permission.
- **AI Use.** Using the Work in commercial AI training datasets, RAG systems, embedding databases, or any automated system that generates outputs derived from the Work requires permission. This restriction applies to the commercial operator of the AI system, not to individual users who paste a recipe into a conversation with a general-purpose AI assistant.

**Clarification on AI:** An individual neighbor asking a general-purpose AI assistant about a recipe from this book is Community Use. A company building a "community building AI coach" that ingests this corpus to generate advice for paying customers is AI Use requiring permission.

## 9. Why this license exists

Standard open-source and Creative Commons licenses were designed for a world where maximum distribution equals maximum benefit. That assumption breaks when platforms can extract and monetize community knowledge at scale without reciprocating.

This license draws a different line. It asks: does your use bring these practices closer to a neighborhood, or further away? The freedoms are broad for the first case. The restrictions exist for the second.

The recipes in this book were developed by neighbors, for neighbors. This license keeps them there.
